## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an elevated, tranquil digital presence for a premier unisex beauty sanctuary. The aesthetic fuses **Soft Luxury**, **Modern Spa Retreatment**, and **Editorial Layout Design**. The interface must evoke serene rejuvenation, tactile warmth, sophisticated self-care, and understated exclusivity. It completely avoids cold minimalism, synthetic brights, and harsh dark-luxury tropes.

### Core Visual Principles
- **Airy Tactility:** Generous negative space, creamy layered washes, and sunlight-dappled composition inspired by editorial lifestyle publications.
- **Organic Asymmetry:** Thoughtfully offset image cards, bespoke arch frames, and deliberate text pairings that break rigid block grids without losing functional clarity.
- **Warm Restraint:** Pure black (#000000) and harsh gray (#808080) are prohibited. All structure relies on deep espresso inks, raw ecru, linen tones, and soft metallic champagne accents.
- **Modern Wellness:** Balances high-fashion grace with approachable, gender-neutral calm, prioritizing legibility and effortless service curation.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from mineral powders, raw silk, sun-dried stone, and botanical clays. Surfaces are rendered in warm, non-reflective undertones to produce a soothing, natural feel.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C9A66B` - Champagne Gold):** Reserved for delicate line art, curated highlights, active state rings, and elevated calls to action.
- **Secondary (`#A8B09F` - Muted Sage):** Employed for treatment badges, botanical accents, wellness tags, and tranquil secondary callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#E9D8D2` - Soft Blush):** Used for soft interactive fills, selected pill states, and delicate highlight backings.
- **Neutral Primary / Text (`#493D35` - Warm Espresso):** The universal ink color. All primary titles, body passages, icons, and defined interactive boundaries use Warm Espresso instead of black.
- **Neutral Muted (`#9D8E82` - Soft Taupe):** Secondary copy, metadata, subtle borders, inactive step markers, and non-distracting dividers.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F8F4EE` - Warm Ivory):** The root background tone across all pages.
- **Surface Elevation (`#EFE6DB` - Soft Sand):** Card panels, elevated overlays, input wells, and segmented controls.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial rhythm, combining the literary romance of a high-contrast serif with the calm, geometric balance of Manrope.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Headlines & Display (EB Garamond):** Display sizes should leverage generous letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) and comfortable leading. Headings should feel like fine magazine mastheads. Italic variants may be selectively deployed for stylistic emphasis in hero phrases or treatment introductions.
- **Body & Functional Labels (Manrope):** Clean, crisp, and comfortable to read. Standard tracking for running text guarantees effortless legibility on both mobile booking flows and expansive desktop screens.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-copy:** Labels set at uppercase should always apply expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to maintain an airy, refined cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model pairs a structured 12-column foundation with asymmetric offsets, granting screens an airy, curated cadence.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 80px maximum column width, 40px (`2.5rem`) gutters, and dynamic outer margins scaling up to 80px (`5rem`). Use 5/7 and 4/8 column pairings to create off-center, editorial tension between images and treatment descriptions.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters with 40px outer margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 16px gutters and 20px outer margins.

### Rhythmic Philosophy
Space is treated as an active architectural element. Section dividers are minimized; transitions between services, testimonials, and gallery pieces are governed by expansive vertical spacing (`space-xl` or larger) to evoke tranquil stillness.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid plastic or digital qualities, visual depth relies on mineral tone layering, translucent washes, and delicate, feather-weight ambient shadows.

### Depth Paradigms
- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces elevate from base `#F8F4EE` (Warm Ivory) to `#EFE6DB` (Soft Sand), providing natural structural hierarchy without requiring stark elevation drops.
- **Sunlit Ambient Shadows:** Shadows emulate indirect natural daylight filtered through linen. They are tinted strictly in Warm Espresso with extreme diffusion:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(73, 61, 53, 0.04)`
  - *Floating Modal / Tray:* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(73, 61, 53, 0.08)`
- **Hairline Outlines:** Interactive borders, cards, and divider rules use a delicate 1px boundary set to `#C9A66B` (Champagne Gold at 25% opacity) or `#9D8E82` (Soft Taupe at 20% opacity). Heavy borders are strictly prohibited.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette combines softened functional corners with graceful organic curves, reflecting architectural archways and sculpted spa spaces.

### Geometry Rules
- **Standard UI Elements:** Interactive buttons, input fields, and standard container panels apply subtle rounded geometry (`0.5rem` to `1rem`).
- **Signature Arch Frames:** Featured portrait photography, artist introductions, and hero promotional cards feature asymmetrical arch curves—such as `border-radius: 120px 120px 8px 8px`—echoing architectural spa alcoves.
- **Pill Formats:** Status chips, service category selectors, and booking step indicators employ full continuous pill geometries for a warm, tactile feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#493D35` (Warm Espresso) with `#F8F4EE` (Warm Ivory) text, subtle 1px border in `#C9A66B` (20% opacity). Hover state shifts surface to `#C9A66B` with `#493D35` text via a soft 250ms ease.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1px border in `#493D35` (Warm Espresso), text in `#493D35`. Hover transitions to `#E9D8D2` (Soft Blush) fill.
- **Text Action:** Manrope Label-LG in `#493D35`, with a persistent hairline underline in `#C9A66B`.

### Service & Ritual Cards
- Enclosed in `#EFE6DB` (Soft Sand) with an imperceptible 1px outline of Taupe (15% opacity).
- Card layouts favor staggered vertical alignment: full-bleed image on top, generous internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`), service duration in Muted Sage pill, price in Champagne Gold, and descriptive prose in Warm Espresso.

### Chips & Filters
- Inactive: `#F8F4EE` surface, 1px border in `#9D8E82` (30% opacity), text in `#9D8E82`.
- Active: `#E9D8D2` (Soft Blush) background, 1px border in `#C9A66B`, text in `#493D35`.

### Form Inputs & Date Picker
- Base field: `#EFE6DB` background, no drop shadow, 1px border in `#9D8E82` (25% opacity). Text in `#493D35`, placeholder in `#9D8E82`.
- Focus state: Outline transitions to 1px `#C9A66B` (Champagne Gold) with a faint halo glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(201, 166, 107, 0.15)`.
- Calendar picker applies custom soft-rounded selection rings with Sage and Blush indicators.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Radios and checkboxes feature a 1.5px border in `#9D8E82`.
- When checked: Background turns `#C9A66B` with a clean ivory checkmark or inner dot. No high-contrast digital blues or greens.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Ritual Timeline:** A delicate Champagne Gold hairline thread guiding clients through multi-step bridal, styling, and unisex wellness itineraries.
- **Aesthetician / Stylist Roster:** Profile cards combining an arch-framed portrait, specialty badges in Muted Sage, and an understated appointment button.
